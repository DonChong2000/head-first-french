Part 1 · Exam check

Part 1 exam check

Thirty-five questions, three tasks, and nobody to blame but your own ears.

This is the test at the end of Part 1. It covers Chapters 1 to 7: sounds, être and avoir, gender, -er verbs, questions, numbers and time, and the small verbs that take you places (aller, venir, faire). Part 1 aims at roughly A1, which is NCLC 1 to 3.

It does two jobs. If you just finished the chapters, it shows you what stuck. If you already know some French, it's your way in: the intro promised that 80% or more lets you jump to Part 2, and this is where you earn it.

Think of it as a test suite for your French. A red line isn't a verdict. It tells you which file to open.

How to take it

  1. Block out 45 to 60 minutes and do it in one sitting. Put your phone on silent.
  2. No peeking. No chapters, no dictionary, no translator, and don't open the answer boxes early. The only person you can fool is you.
  3. Use headphones for Section A. Try to play each clip twice at most. Real conversations don't rewind.
  4. Click Check at the end of Sections A, B and C. Then do the writing and speaking tasks (D and E) on paper or in a notes app. Those two have no automatic score.
  5. Write down your three scores before you open the Scoring box at the bottom.

Watch it!

Your score is a self-check, not a prediction. It does not tell you what you would get on TEF Canada or TCF Canada. Those tests are made by other people, with other questions, and they are scored their own way. Check the official TEF Canada and TCF Canada pages for how they work.

Nothing here copies a real exam. The sections follow the four skills those tests are built around (listening, reading, writing, speaking). We added a grammar section, because grammar is where Part 1 pays off fastest.

中文腦：考試靠細字

中文表達複數同時態，多數靠量詞、時間詞同語氣詞，例如「我食咗飯」(zo2) / 「我吃了飯」(le)，「書」本身唔變。法文就將呢啲資訊收入好細嘅字入面，而且字尾好多時聽唔到。所以聽力要專登留意：le 定 les、un 定 des、ils sont（s）定 ils‿ont（z）。

聽到 vient de 加動詞，當佢係「啱啱…」(ngaam1 ngaam1 / 剛剛 gānggāng)。聽到 va 加動詞，當佢係「就快…」(zau6 faai3 / 即將 jíjiāng)。呢兩個只係大概對應，但係夠你喺聽力入面揀啱答案。

聽到數字、時間、價錢，即刻用阿拉伯數字寫低，唔好喺腦入面翻譯。寫作交卷之前檢查三樣：冠詞、-e／-s／-nt 字尾、重音符號。

Section A · Compréhension orale

Ten clips, ten questions. The French is hidden on purpose, so you can't read your way to the answer. Click ▶, read the question, pick one option. Chapters 1 and 2 told you where the points hide: the pronoun, the tiny word, the z.

Section A: listen and choose

  1. How much does it cost?
  2. When does the appointment start?
  3. What does the speaker say?
  4. The speaker is asking...
  5. What is the situation?
  6. What is the situation?
  7. Who is the speaker talking to?
  8. What is being described?
  9. What does the speaker have?
  10. When is the meeting?
Answers and what to revisit
  1. Ça fait douze dollars cinquante, s'il vous plaît. 12,50 $. (Ch 6)
  2. Le rendez-vous commence à trois heures et demie. 3 h 30. (Ch 6)
  3. Ils ont des amis au bureau de Montréal. ont with the z of the liaison, so "they have". (Ch 2, and the z comes from Ch 1)
  4. Pourquoi est-ce que vous cherchez un nouvel appartement ? pourquoi means why. (Ch 5)
  5. Elle va téléphoner au propriétaire demain. va plus an infinitive: she is going to call. (Ch 7)
  6. Non merci, nous venons de manger au restaurant. venons de plus an infinitive: we just ate. (Ch 7)
  7. Salut Marie, tu manges avec nous à midi ? salut and tu say it's someone close. (Ch 2)
  8. Les chambres sont petites, mais elles sont calmes. les, sont and elles carry the plural, because the final -s is silent. (Ch 3, and Ch 1 for the silent endings)
  9. Je n'ai pas de voiture, mais j'ai un vélo. n'ai pas de is the "no", j'ai is the "yes". (Ch 4)
  10. La réunion est le quinze mars à neuf heures. The date is 15 March, and the f of neuf sounds like a v before heures. (Ch 6, with a liaison from Ch 1)

Section B · Compréhension écrite

Two short texts, five questions each. You don't need every word. Hunt for the details the questions ask about, like you did in Chapter 3. You can click any sentence to hear it, but try reading first.

Section B: read and choose

Text 1: an apartment ad

Appartement à louer. Beau 3 1/2 lumineux dans un quartier calme de Québec. Il y a une grande chambre, une petite cuisine et un balcon. Le chauffage est inclus, mais l'eau chaude n'est pas incluse. Le loyer est de 780 $ par mois. Pas de chats, s'il vous plaît. Le logement est libre le premier septembre. Pour visiter, téléphonez à M. Gagnon après 18 h.

  1. How much is the rent?
  2. When can you move in?
  3. What is not included in the rent?
  4. Which description is right?
  5. When should you call to visit?

Text 2: an email from a coworker

Bonjour Marc,
Je viens de parler à Mme Roy. La réunion de demain commence à neuf heures et demie, pas à dix heures. Nous allons parler du nouveau projet dans la salle 3. Attention : il fait très froid dans le bureau ! Moi, je n'ai pas de voiture, alors j'arrive en autobus. Est-ce que tu manges avec nous à midi ? Léa et moi, nous allons au restaurant.
À demain,
Julie

  1. What time does the meeting start?
  2. What did Julie do a moment ago?
  3. What is Julie's warning about?
  4. How does Julie get to work?
  5. What does Julie ask Marc?
Answers and what to revisit
  1. Le loyer est de 780 $ par mois. 780 $ a month. (Ch 6)
  2. libre le premier septembre: September 1. (Ch 6)
  3. l'eau chaude n'est pas incluse: hot water. Notice incluse with an -e, because eau is feminine. (Ch 3, and Ch 4 for the negation)
  4. une grande chambre, une petite cuisine. Size adjectives go before the noun. (Ch 3)
  5. après 18 h: after 6 pm. (Ch 6)
  6. neuf heures et demie, pas à dix heures: 9 h 30. (Ch 6)
  7. Je viens de parler à Mme Roy. viens de plus an infinitive means "just did". (Ch 7)
  8. il fait très froid dans le bureau: the office is cold. (Ch 7)
  9. je n'ai pas de voiture, alors j'arrive en autobus: she has no car, so she comes by bus. (Ch 4)
  10. Est-ce que tu manges avec nous à midi ? A yes/no question with est-ce que. (Ch 5)

Fifteen quick items from Chapters 2 to 7. Type or choose. Accents count: if your answer is right apart from an accent, the checker flags it as a slip and doesn't give you the point.

Section C: grammar and vocabulary

  1. Nous très faim.
  2. Marie est comptable.
  3. Je cherche chambre calme.
  4. une cuisine (lumineux)
  5. Nous à huit heures. (commencer)
  6. Je voiture. (I don't have a car)
  7. J'aime café.
  8. Which is the correct formal question?
  9. est l'adresse ?
  10. Tu n'aimes pas la neige ? , j'adore la neige !
  11. 65 in words:
  12. 1 May in words:
  13. Je vais banque.
  14. Marc du bureau. (venir)
  15. À Montréal, en hiver, il très froid. (faire)
Answers and what to revisit
  1. Nous avons très faim. Hunger is something you have. (Ch 2)
  2. Marie est comptable. No article before a job after être. (Ch 2)
  3. une chambre. chambre is feminine, and calme comes after it. (Ch 3)
  4. lumineuse. -eux becomes -euse. (Ch 3)
  5. commençons. The ç keeps the c soft before -ons. (Ch 4)
  6. n'ai pas de. Elision, then une shrinks to de after a no. (Ch 4)
  7. J'aime le café. A like takes the definite article. (Ch 4)
  8. Parle-t-il anglais ? Verb, then -t- (parle ends in an e), then the pronoun. (Ch 5)
  9. Quelle est l'adresse ? adresse is feminine. (Ch 5, and Ch 3 for the gender)
  10. Si, j'adore la neige ! si says yes to a negative question. (Ch 5)
  11. soixante-cinq. (Ch 6)
  12. le premier mai. The first of the month is premier, not "un". (Ch 6)
  13. Je vais à la banque. banque is feminine, so à la. (Ch 7, and Ch 3 for the gender)
  14. Marc vient du bureau. venir, plus du for de le. (Ch 7)
  15. il fait très froid. Weather uses faire. (Ch 7)

Section D · Expression écrite

This one has no automatic score. You write, then you grade yourself with the rubric. Be honest. The rubric is a mirror, not a judge.

Section D: write a message

Click the task to hear it:

Vous venez de louer un appartement. Écrivez un message de 60 à 80 mots à votre nouveau propriétaire. Présentez-vous et posez trois questions.

In English: you've just rented a flat. Write 60 to 80 words to your new landlord. Introduce yourself and ask three questions. Use paper or a notes app, and give yourself 15 minutes. (That limit is ours, not an exam rule.) Count your words at the end.

Self-check rubric and a model answer

Answer yes or no to each point. Six yeses or more is a good result for Part 1.

  • Did you open with Bonjour plus madame or monsieur, and close with Merci?
  • Did you use vous with the landlord, never tu?
  • Did you give your name, where you're from and your age, with J'ai ... ans and not Je suis ... ans?
  • Did you leave out the article before your job or nationality (Je suis ingénieure)?
  • Are there three real questions, each built with est-ce que, inversion or a question word, and each ending in a question mark?
  • Did you elide where you had to (j'habite, j'ai, n'ai) and write the verb endings by pattern?
  • Do the adjectives agree, and are the accents in place?
  • Is the message 60 to 80 words long?

A model answer (about 70 words). Click it to hear it.

Bonjour madame Gagnon,
Je m'appelle Ka-yan Chan. Je suis ingénieure et je viens de Hong Kong. J'ai trente ans et j'ai un chat. Je travaille dans un hôtel. Je ne fume pas. J'habite à Vancouver, mais je vais habiter à Québec en septembre. Est-ce que le chauffage est inclus ? Quel est le loyer par mois ? Est-ce que vous acceptez les chats ?
Merci beaucoup,
Ka-yan Chan

Section E · Expression orale

Two speaking tasks. Record yourself with the 🎙 button in each task and play it back. You are the grader, so listen the way a stranger would: could they follow you without effort?

Section E, task 1: introduce yourself

Click the task to hear it:

Présentez-vous.

Talk for one minute, with no notes. (That time is ours, not an exam rule.) Say who you are, where you're from and where you live, your age, your job or studies, the languages you speak, and two things you like. Chapter 2 gave you the frame, and now you add the rest.

Self-check rubric and a model answer

Answer yes or no to each point. Six yeses or more is a good result for Part 1.

  • Did you start with Bonjour and Je m'appelle?
  • Did you say your age with J'ai ... ans?
  • Did you leave out the article before your job or nationality?
  • Did you say where you're from (Je viens de) and where you live (J'habite à)?
  • Did you say at least one thing you like, with le, la or les (J'aime la musique)?
  • Did you elide (j'ai, j'habite) and keep the silent endings silent?
  • Did you hear a French u (not ou), no tongue touching in the nasals, and the R in your throat?
  • Did you keep going for the whole minute without switching to English?

A model answer. Click it, then say it after the voice.

Bonjour ! Je m'appelle Ka-yan Chan. J'ai trente ans et je viens de Hong Kong. Je suis ingénieure, mais je cherche un emploi à Québec. J'habite à Vancouver avec mon chat. Je parle cantonais, mandarin et anglais, et j'étudie le français. J'aime la musique, j'aime manger au restaurant et j'adore la neige. En septembre, je vais habiter à Québec. Merci !

Section E, task 2: phone the advertiser

Here's the ad. Click it to hear it:

Chambre à louer dans une maison à Laval. Libre le premier octobre. Grande cuisine. Téléphonez après 18 h.

You're on the phone with the person who placed the ad. Ask eight different questions, out loud, with vous. The ad already answers some things, so ask about what it leaves out. Say them aloud and record yourself.

Self-check rubric and model questions

Answer yes or no to each point. Six yeses or more is a good result for Part 1.

  • Did you open with Bonjour and use vous every time?
  • Did you ask eight different questions, none of them already answered by the ad?
  • Did you use at least four different question words or frames (est-ce que, quel, combien, où, quand, comment)?
  • Did est-ce que become est-ce qu' before il, elle, on and ils?
  • Did quel agree with its noun?
  • Did your voice rise on yes/no questions, and stay level or fall on questions with a question word?
  • Did you thank the person and say au revoir at the end?
  • Did you sound clear when you played it back, with no long freezes?

Model questions. Yours will differ, and that's fine.

  1. Bonjour, madame. Quel est le loyer, s'il vous plaît ?
  2. Quelle est l'adresse ?
  3. Est-ce que le chauffage est inclus dans le loyer ?
  4. Combien de personnes habitent dans la maison ?
  5. Est-ce que la maison est loin du métro ?
  6. Est-ce que vous acceptez les chats ?
  7. Quand est-ce que je peux visiter la chambre ?
  8. Comment vous appelez-vous ?

Merci beaucoup, madame. Au revoir !

Your score

Add up Sections A, B and C. The most you can get is 35 (10 + 10 + 15).

  • 28 or more (80%): go to Part 2.
  • 20 to 27: redo the chapters you missed (the table below tells you which ones), do their exercises again, then retake this check in a week.
  • Under 20: restart from Chapter 2 and do the exercises. Don't be gloomy about it. Chapter 1 stays done, and the second pass is faster than the first.

Sections D and E have no number. If you got fewer than six yeses on any rubric, redo that task before you move on, even if you scored 28 or more. And remember that this is your own yardstick, not a TEF or TCF score.

BULLET POINTS

  • Part 1 gave you the sounds: silent endings, u against ou, nasals, the throat R, elision and liaison.
  • You can say who you are, how old you are and how you feel with être and avoir, and you know which of the two to pick.
  • Nouns come with a gender, articles and adjectives follow it, and the short list of adjectives goes before the noun.
  • -er verbs, ne ... pas and de after a no work as one machine. Questions use est-ce que, inversion or a question word, and si answers a negative question.
  • Numbers up to 1000, the time, dates and prices are enough to rent a room and be on time for the visit.
  • aller, faire and venir give you the near future (je vais manger) and the recent past (je viens d'arriver), plus places with au, à la and aux.
  • Part 2 brings the past tenses, where être and avoir start working as helpers. Everything you just tested is what they stand on.
  • 28 out of 35 or better: go. Less: the table says where to look. You can retake this check as often as you like.
