Head First French

a.k.a. Tête la première

French from zero to NCLC 7, for people who already think in English and Chinese.

What is this, really?

A joke that got out of hand. Programmers learn a new language from a Head First book, so this is one for French: same boxes, same cheesy exercises, different compiler. Silent letters are commented-out code, liaison is a linker, and grammatical gender is a type system nobody asked for.

It's a personal study project, shared with friends for free. It is not commercial, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by O'Reilly Media, who own the Head First name. If you like the format, buy one of their real books.

It's also not official exam material. A tutor and a real practice test still beat any book.

This course is for one kind of reader. Your English is good (around CLB 7 to 9), you grew up speaking Cantonese or Mandarin, and you need French at NCLC 7 on TEF Canada or TCF Canada. That's the level that earns French points in Express Entry.

You'll lean on both languages you already have. English hands you thousands of words for free, because about a third of English vocabulary came from French. Chinese hands you some sounds English speakers struggle with, like the u in tu, which is the vowel in 書 (syu1) or 魚 (yú).

Click any mot en rouge to hear it. Start with How to use this book.
